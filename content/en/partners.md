## FAQ partenaires

- **Who can become a Govern One partner?** : Any organization engaged in governance, risk, compliance, technology or training can become a partner. Whether you are a consulting firm, an academic institution, a technology integrator or a regulatory organization, we welcome partners who share our vision of digital transformation of governance in Africa.
- **Is the partnership paid?** : No, access to the partner network is free. Only certain premium programs are optional and offer additional benefits such as certifying training, priority support and exclusive co-marketing opportunities.
- **Can I propose my own training or modules?** : Yes, our Partner Impact Lab program promotes co-creation and knowledge sharing. You can propose your own training, modules or solutions that will be integrated into our ecosystem and shared with the partner community.
- **What are the benefits of the partnership program?** : Partners benefit from privileged access to our solutions, training opportunities, visibility on our platform, joint calls for tenders, and a partner network to develop their activities in Africa.
- **How can I join the partnership program?** : To join our partnership program, simply fill out the application form available on this page. Our team will review your application and contact you as soon as possible to discuss your integration into our ecosystem.
- **What type of technical support is provided to partners?** : We provide comprehensive technical support including detailed documentation, technical training, access to our API, dedicated support and co-development sessions to help you integrate Govern One into your solutions.
