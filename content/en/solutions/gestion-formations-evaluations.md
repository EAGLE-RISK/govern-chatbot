A complete suite of tools to optimize your training and evaluations
Powerful and intuitive solutions for every aspect of training management

## Éléments

- **Training Planning** : Planning and organization of training with session, trainer and participant management for optimal organization.
- **Evaluation Tracking** : Creation and tracking of evaluations with customized questionnaires, results and analyses for complete follow-up.
- **Dashboards** : Real-time dashboards on training, participation, results and progress for complete visibility.
- **Calendar Management** : Training calendar management with automatic notifications and reminders for effective organization.
- **Resource Library** : Centralized library of training resources with documents, videos and materials for easy access.
- **Certifications & Certificates** : Automatic generation of training certificates and attestations with validation and archiving for complete traceability.
