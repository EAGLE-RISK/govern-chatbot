# Technical FAQ

Answers to the most frequently asked technical questions. Troubleshooting, common errors and solutions.

## Introduction

This technical FAQ answers the most frequently asked questions by Govern One users.

**Question categories:**
- Connection issues
- Common errors
- Configuration
- Performance
- Integrations

## Connection issues

**I cannot log in to my account**

Possible solutions:
1. Check your credentials (email and password)
2. Make sure your account is activated
3. Check your internet connection
4. Try resetting your password

**My account is locked**

Contact technical support to unlock your account.

## Common errors

**Error 404 - Page not found**

This error indicates that the requested page does not exist. Check the URL or contact support.

**Error 500 - Server error**

This error is temporary. Try again in a few moments. If the problem persists, contact support.

**Loading error**

Clear your browser cache and try again.

## Performance issues

**The platform is slow**

Solutions:
- Check your internet connection
- Close unnecessary tabs
- Clear browser cache
- Contact support if the problem persists

**Reports take time to generate**

This is normal for complex reports. Generation can take several minutes depending on the amount of data.
