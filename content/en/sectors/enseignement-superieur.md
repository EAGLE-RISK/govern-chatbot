# Higher Education

Manage academic compliance, student data protection and governance with solutions adapted to higher education institutions.

## Vue d'ensemble

Higher education institutions must manage complex challenges in academic compliance, student data protection and governance. Govern One offers solutions to manage these challenges effectively.

## Enjeux du secteur

- **Student data protection** : Secure student personal data and comply with GDPR and FERPA.
- **Academic compliance** : Comply with academic regulations, accreditations and quality standards.
- **Personnel management** : Manage training, certification and compliance of academic and administrative personnel.
- **Campus security** : Ensure the security of students, staff and campus facilities.
- **Operational risks** : Manage risks related to operations, academic continuity and incident management.
- **Academic quality** : Maintain high standards of academic quality and teaching excellence.
## Solutions proposées

- **Student data protection** : Secure student data with robust data protection controls.
- **Academic compliance** : Manage your compliance with academic regulations with automated processes.
- **Personnel management** : Manage training, certification and compliance of your personnel.
- **Campus security** : Protect your campus with robust security processes.
