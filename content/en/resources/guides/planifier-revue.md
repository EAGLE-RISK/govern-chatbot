# Schedule a Review

Organize periodic reviews to maintain risk relevance.

- **Niveau :** Advanced
- **Durée de lecture :** 25 min
- **Tags :** Review, Periodic, Validation


## What is a Review?

A **review** is a periodic assessment of risks to:
- Verify their relevance
- Update assessments
- Validate mitigation actions

## Steps to Schedule a Review

**Location** : Risk Management → Reviews → Create

**Step 1: General Information**
1. Click **"Create a review"**
2. Fill in the fields:
   - **Title** : Ex: "Semi-annual operational risks review"
   - **Description** : Review description
   - **Review type** :
     - Periodic review
     - Ad-hoc review
   - **Period** : Period covered by the review

**Step 2: Risk Selection**
1. Select risks to review
2. You can filter by:
   - Category
   - Status
   - Owner
   - Activity

**Step 3: Planning**
1. **Start date** : Start date
2. **End date** : Closing date
3. **Responsible** : Person in charge
4. **Participants** : Involved people

**Step 4: Save**
1. Verify information
2. Click **"Create the review"**

## Conduct a Review

During the review:

1. For each risk:
   - Verify relevance
   - Reassess if necessary
   - Update information
   - Validate mitigation actions
2. Document decisions
3. Close the review
