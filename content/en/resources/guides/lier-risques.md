# Link Risks

Identify and manage dependencies between risks for a global view.

- **Niveau :** Advanced
- **Durée de lecture :** 20 min
- **Tags :** Linking, Dependencies, Relations


## Why Link Risks?

Linking risks allows to:
- Identify dependencies between risks
- Understand risk cascades
- Manage correlated risks

## Steps to Link Risks

**Location** : Risk Management → Risk Links

**Method 1: From a Risk**
1. Access a risk detail
2. Click on the **"Links"** tab
3. Click **"Add a link"**
4. Select the risk to link
5. Define the link type:
   - **Cause → Effect** : This risk causes the other
   - **Effect → Cause** : This risk is caused by the other
   - **Correlation** : Risks are correlated
6. Save

**Method 2: From the Links Page**
1. Go to **Risk Links**
2. Click **"Create a link"**
3. Select the two risks
4. Define the link type
5. Save
