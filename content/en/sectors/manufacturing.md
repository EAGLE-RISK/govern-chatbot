# Manufacturing

Manage quality, safety and compliance in your factories with solutions adapted to the manufacturing sector.

## Vue d'ensemble

The manufacturing sector faces complex challenges in quality, safety, regulatory compliance and supply chain management. Govern One offers solutions to manage these challenges effectively.

## Enjeux du secteur

- **Quality control** : Ensure consistent product quality and compliance with industrial standards.
- **Industrial safety** : Prevent accidents and ensure worker safety in factories.
- **Environmental compliance** : Comply with environmental regulations and reduce ecological impact.
- **Supplier management** : Monitor and manage compliance of multiple suppliers and subcontractors.
- **Regulatory compliance** : Comply with ISO standards, product safety regulations and labor codes.
- **Operational risks** : Manage risks related to production, maintenance and business continuity.
## Solutions proposées

- **Quality management** : Complete platform to manage product quality and compliance with standards.
- **Industrial safety** : Protect your employees with robust safety processes and continuous training.
- **Environmental compliance** : Monitor and report your environmental compliance with automated tools.
- **Supply chain management** : Monitor and manage compliance of your suppliers and subcontractors.
