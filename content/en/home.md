## Éléments

- **Governance** : Structure your governance bodies, define roles and responsibilities, and ensure decision transparency.
- **Risk Management** : Identify, assess and control operational, financial and strategic risks with an adapted approach.
- **Compliance – Standards & Frameworks** : Align your compliance requirements with standards (ISO, frameworks) and harmonize your policies and procedures.
- **Internal control** : Define controls, track execution and centralize evidence to secure your processes and audits.
- **Compliance – Anti-corruption** : Strengthen integrity: third-party governance, controls, training and alert handling to prevent corruption.
- **Compliance – ECOWAS / CEMAC obligations** : Manage regional obligations, structure your controls and produce the reporting expected by authorities.
- **Compliance – AML / CFT / PF** : Centralize KYC, screening and monitoring to detect money laundering, terrorist financing and proliferation risks.
