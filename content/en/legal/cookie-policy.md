# Cookie Policy

**Dernière mise à jour :** January 1, 2025




## What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small text file stored on your computer, tablet or smartphone when you visit a website. Cookies allow the site to recognize your device and remember certain information about your preferences.


## How we use cookies

Govern One uses cookies to improve your experience on our website, analyze the use of our services and personalize content. We use different types of cookies:


## Types of cookies used


### Strictly necessary cookies


## Éléments

- Session cookies to maintain your connection
- Security cookies to protect against attacks
- Language preference cookies
- Shopping cart cookies
### Performance cookies


## Éléments

- Google Analytics to analyze traffic
- Audience measurement cookies
- A/B test cookies
- Error monitoring cookies
### Functionality cookies


## Éléments

- Display preferences
- Notification settings
- Content preferences
- Geolocation cookies
### Targeting cookies


## Éléments

- Advertising cookies
- Social media cookies
- Remarketing cookies
- Advertising partner cookies
## Third-party cookies


## Éléments

- Google Analytics — Traffic and user behavior analysis
- Google Maps — Interactive map display
- Social networks — Share buttons and widgets
- Chat services — Online customer support
## Retention period


## Éléments

- Session cookies — Deleted when the browser closes
- Persistent cookies — Retained for a defined period (1 month to 2 years)
- Preference cookies — Retained until preferences are modified
## Managing your preferences


### Browser settings


## Éléments

- Chrome — Settings > Privacy and security > Cookies
- Firefox — Options > Privacy and security > Cookies
- Safari — Preferences > Privacy > Cookies
- Edge — Settings > Cookies and site permissions
