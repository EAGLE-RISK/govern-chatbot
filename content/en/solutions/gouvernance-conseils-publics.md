# Governance for Public Boards & Elected Officials: Enhanced transparency and efficiency

**Catégorie :** Governance & Compliance

Streamline the preparation of school board and local government meetings, collaborate securely and ensure transparency with a solution dedicated to the public sector.

## Fonctionnalités

A solution specially designed for public boards and elected officials
Powerful and intuitive solutions for every aspect of public governance

## Éléments

- **Public Meeting Management** : Preparation and organization of meetings with respect for transparency obligations, automatic publication of agendas and minutes.
- **Enhanced Transparency** : Public portal for access to documents and decisions with compliance with transparency and information access requirements.
- **Regulatory Compliance** : Compliance with legal obligations with deadline tracking, automatic alerts and complete documentation for audits.
- **Elected Official Management** : Tracking of mandates, conflict of interest declarations and ethical obligations with automatic reminders and centralized documentation.
- **Secure Archiving** : Compliant archiving of official documents with long-term retention and controlled access according to public archiving rules.
- **Public Reporting** : Automatic generation of public reports with visualizations and exports for citizen communication and accountability.
