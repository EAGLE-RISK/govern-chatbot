# Retail

Manage operational risks, compliance and customer data protection with solutions adapted to retail.

## Vue d'ensemble

Retail is evolving rapidly with e-commerce and new consumer expectations. Govern One offers solutions to manage risks, compliance and data protection in this dynamic sector.

## Enjeux du secteur

- **Customer data protection** : Secure personal and payment data in accordance with GDPR and PCI-DSS.
- **Supply chain management** : Monitor and manage risks in the complex supply chain.
- **Employee compliance** : Manage training, certification and compliance of a large number of employees.
- **Operational risks** : Identify and mitigate risks related to daily store operations.
- **Regulatory compliance** : Comply with regulations on product safety, labeling and sales.
- **Store security** : Ensure the security of stores, employees and customers.
