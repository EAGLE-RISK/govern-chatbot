# Digital Services Act

**Dernière mise à jour :** January 1, 2025




## Introduction

This page describes how Govern One complies with the European Union's Digital Services Act (DSA), which governs digital services and the responsibility of online platforms.


## Our status under the DSA

Govern One operates as an intermediary digital service, providing governance and compliance solutions. We are committed to complying with all applicable obligations of the Digital Services Act.


## Transparency and accountability


### Content transparency


## Éléments

- Our content moderation policies
- Criteria used for content recommendations
- Measures taken against illegal content
- Content removal statistics
### User responsibility


## Éléments

- Respecting terms of use
- Not publishing illegal content
- Reporting problematic content
- Respecting the rights of others
## Content moderation


### Reporting system

We have implemented a reporting system allowing users to report content that violates our terms of use or the law.


### Moderation process


## Éléments

- Quick review of reports (within 24 hours)
- Evaluation by our moderation team
- Removal of illegal content
- Notification to concerned users
- Right to appeal
## Minors protection


## Éléments

- Appropriate age verification
- Parental controls available
- Access restrictions to sensitive content
- Priority reporting of content harmful to minors
## Advertising and recommendations


### Advertising transparency


## Éléments

- Advertiser identification
- Information on advertising targeting
- Ability to disable targeting
- Access to data used for targeting
### Recommendation algorithms


## Éléments

- Respect content diversity
- Avoid creating filter bubbles
- Allow users to understand recommendations
- Offer personalization options
## Data access


## Éléments

- Academic research on online security
- Regulatory authority investigations
- Legal requests from competent authorities
- Compliance audits
## Abuse measures


## Éléments

- Automatic detection of fraudulent accounts
- Monitoring of suspicious activities
- Cooperation with competent authorities
- Ongoing training of our team
## Recourse and mediation


## Éléments

- Internal complaint process
- Mediation by an independent third party
- Recourse to competent authorities
- Arbitration according to EU rules
## Cooperation with authorities


## Éléments

- National regulatory authorities
- European Digital Services Council
- Competent law enforcement
- Consumer protection organizations
## Updates and compliance

We are committed to maintaining our DSA compliance and updating our practices based on the evolution of European regulations.
