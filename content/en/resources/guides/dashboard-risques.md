# Risk Dashboard

Understand and use the risk dashboard for a complete overview.

- **Niveau :** Intermediate
- **Durée de lecture :** 20 min
- **Tags :** Dashboard, Overview, Statistics


## Access the Dashboard

**Location** : Risk Management → Dashboard

The dashboard displays several sections:

**1. Overview**
- Total number of risks
- Distribution by status (Draft, Under evaluation, Validated, Closed)
- Distribution by criticality (Very low, Low, Medium, High, Very high)
- Trend charts

**2. Statistics by Category**
- Strategic risks
- Financial risks
- Operational risks
- Market risks
- Technological risks
- Project risks
- Supply chain risks

**3. Quick Actions**
- Create a new risk
- Create a control
- Record an incident
- Schedule an assessment

**4. Recent Risks**
- List of last created or modified risks
- Quick access to details

## Customize the Dashboard

You can filter the dashboard by:
- Period (7 days, 30 days, 3 months, 1 year)
- Risk status
- Risk category
- Risk owner
- Activity/Department
