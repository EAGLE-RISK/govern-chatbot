# An actionable internal control system


Controls that actually run. Evidence ready on audit day. Zero improvisation.

**Catégorie :** Internal control

## Fonctionnalités

A complete framework for internal control
Clear tools to secure your processes and audits.

## Fonctionnalités détaillées

- **Control library** : Controls organized by process, risk, and entity.
- **Execution & ownership** : Owners, frequencies, and execution tracking.
- **Testing & assessments** : Test planning and effectiveness measurement.
- **Evidence & documentation** : Artifacts centralized, versioned, and auditable.
- **Gaps & remediation** : Deficiencies prioritized and action plans tracked.
- **Reporting** : Dashboards and exports for leadership and audit.
