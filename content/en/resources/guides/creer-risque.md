# Create a Risk

Complete process to create and assess a risk in Govern One.

- **Niveau :** Intermediate
- **Durée de lecture :** 30 min
- **Tags :** Risk, Creation, Assessment


## Overview

Creating a risk in Govern One follows a structured process that captures all necessary information.

**Location** : Risk Management → Risks → Create

## Detailed Steps

**Step 1: Basic Information**
1. Click **"Create a risk"**or **"Add a risk"**
2. Fill in the form:

**Required fields**:
- **Risk name**: Clear and descriptive title
- Example: "Cybersecurity risk on IT systems"
- **Description**: Detailed description of the risk
- Include: nature, potential causes, context
- **Risk profile**: Select an existing profile
- **Activity**: Concerned department/activity
- **Risk type**: Category (strategic, financial, operational, etc.)

**Optional fields**:
- **Parent risk**: If this risk is related to another risk
- **End date**: If the risk has a limited duration
- **Associated users**: People involved in risk management

3. Click **"Next"**

**Step 2: Risk Assessment (Heatmap)**
1. **Select Impact**:
- Click on a heatmap cell
- Impact represents the consequences if the risk materializes
- Levels: Very low, Low, Medium, High, Very high

2. **Select Probability**:
- Probability represents the frequency of occurrence
- Levels: Very low, Low, Medium, High, Very high

3. **Automatic Score**:
- Score is calculated automatically: Score = Impact × Probability
- Cell color indicates criticality

4. **Visualization**:
- The heatmap visually displays all existing risks
- Red zones indicate critical risks
- Green zones indicate low risks

**Step 3: Owner and Responsible Parties**
1. **Risk owner**:
- Default: You (logged-in user)
- Can be modified if necessary

2. **Associated users**:
- Add people who need to be informed
- They will receive notifications

**Step 4: Save**
1. Verify all information
2. Click **"Create the risk"**
3. The risk is created with status **"Draft"**

> **Tip**: Use risk templates from catalogs to save time.

## After Creation

Once the risk is created, you can:
- Add controls
- Plan mitigation actions
- Create KRI indicators
- Link to other risks
- Change status (Under evaluation → Validated)
