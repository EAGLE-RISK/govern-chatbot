# Supplier Risk Management: Monitor and manage your third-party ecosystem

**Catégorie :** Risk Management

Integrated risk platform to monitor and manage your third-party supplier landscape. Assess, monitor and mitigate risks related to your partners and suppliers with a complete solution.

## Fonctionnalités

A complete solution to manage your supplier risks
Powerful and intuitive solutions for every aspect of supplier risk management

## Éléments

- **Supplier Inventory** : Centralized directory of all your suppliers with classification by category, criticality and risk level.
- **Risk Assessment** : Complete supplier risk assessment with customizable questionnaires, automatic scoring and risk matrices.
- **Continuous Monitoring** : Real-time supplier monitoring with automatic alerts on risk changes and critical events.
- **Contract Management** : Contract and obligation tracking with deadlines, renewals and compliance with contractual requirements.
- **Compliance & Due Diligence** : Due diligence process with compliance checks, certifications and supplier audits.
- **Reporting & Analytics** : Detailed reports on supplier landscape with visualizations, trends and risk concentration analyses.
