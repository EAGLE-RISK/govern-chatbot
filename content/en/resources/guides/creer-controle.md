# Create a Control

Implement controls to reduce risks and test their effectiveness.

- **Niveau :** Intermediate
- **Durée de lecture :** 25 min
- **Tags :** Control, Mitigation, Test


## What is a Control?

A **control** is a measure implemented to:
- Reduce the probability of risk occurrence
- Reduce impact if the risk materializes
- Detect risk occurrence

## Steps to Create a Control

**Location** : Risk Management → Controls → Create

**Step 1: Basic Information**
1. Click **"Create a control"**
2. Fill in the fields:
   - **Control name** : Ex: "System access control"
   - **Description** : Detailed description of the control
   - **Associated risk** : Select the concerned risk
   - **Control type** :
     - Preventive: Prevents occurrence
     - Detective: Detects occurrence
     - Corrective: Corrects after occurrence
   - **Owner** : Control responsible

**Step 2: Configuration**
1. **Test frequency** :
   - Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Annual
2. **Test method** :
   - Automatic, Manual, Mixed
3. **Expected effectiveness** :
   - Very low, Low, Medium, High, Very high

**Step 3: Save**
1. Verify information
2. Click **"Create the control"**

## Test a Control

Once created, you can test the control's effectiveness:

1. Access the control
2. Click **"Test"**
3. Fill in the test form:
- Test date
- Result (Effective, Partially effective, Not effective)
- Observations
- Evidence/Justifications (attached files)
4. Save the test

> **Tip**: Regularly test your controls to ensure their continued effectiveness.
