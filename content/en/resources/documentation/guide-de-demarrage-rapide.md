# Quick Start Guide

Learn the basics of the Govern One platform in a few simple steps. Perfect for new users.

## Introduction

Welcome to the Govern One Quick Start Guide. This guide will walk you through your first steps with the platform.

**Govern One** is a comprehensive governance, risk, and compliance (GRC) management platform designed to simplify and optimize your organizational processes.

In this guide, you will learn:
- How to create your account
- How to set up your profile
- Basic features
- How to create your first project

## Creating your account

To get started with Govern One, you first need to create an account.

**Steps:**
1. Go to the registration page
2. Fill out the form with your information
3. Verify your email
4. Log in to your account

Once your account is created, you will receive a confirmation email with instructions to activate your account.

## Setting up your profile

After creating your account, set up your profile to personalize your experience.

**Information to configure:**
- First and last name
- Profile picture
- Notification preferences
- Language and timezone

These settings can be changed at any time from your account settings.

## Your first steps

Now that your account is set up, let's explore the basic features.

**Dashboard:**
The dashboard is your main entry point. It gives you an overview of your projects, tasks, and notifications.

**Navigation:**
Use the side menu to access different sections of the platform.

**Creating a project:**
Click on "New Project" to create your first governance project.
