# Quick Start Guide

First login and interface exploration. Learn the basics in a few minutes.

- **Niveau :** Beginner
- **Durée de lecture :** 15 min
- **Tags :** Getting Started, First Steps, Login


## First Login

#### Step 1: Access the Platform
1. Open your browser and go to the Govern One URL
2. Click **"Sign in"**or **"Login"**
3. Enter your credentials (email and password)
4. Click **"Login"**

> **Tip**: If you received an email invitation, use the provided link to access your account directly.

#### Step 2: Complete Your Profile
On your first login, you may need to complete your profile:
1. Verify your personal information
2. Add your phone number if necessary
3. Select your business sector
4. Click **"Save"**

#### Step 3: Explore the Dashboard
Once logged in, you access the **main dashboard**which displays:
- Overview of your risks
- Key statistics
- Quick actions
- Links to main features

## Interface Navigation

#### Main Menu
The side menu (sidebar) contains the main sections:
- **Dashboard**: Overview
- **Risk Management**: Main module
- **Settings**: Configuration
- **Administration**: User management (if you have the rights)

#### Search Bar
Use the search bar at the top to:
- Search for risks, controls, incidents
- Quickly access features
- Navigate to specific pages
