# Real Estate & Construction

Manage project risks, regulatory compliance and site safety with solutions adapted to the construction sector.

## Vue d'ensemble

The real estate and construction sector faces complex challenges in project management, site safety and regulatory compliance. Govern One offers solutions to manage these challenges effectively.

## Enjeux du secteur

- **Site safety** : Prevent accidents and ensure worker safety on construction sites.
- **Project management** : Manage deadlines, budgets and risks of complex projects.
- **Regulatory compliance** : Comply with building codes, permits and local regulations.
- **Subcontractor management** : Monitor and manage compliance of multiple subcontractors and suppliers.
- **Financial risks** : Manage risks related to costs, delays and contractual disputes.
- **Quality and inspection** : Ensure work quality and compliance with specifications.
