# Entity & Subsidiary Management: Centralize your corporate records

**Catégorie :** Governance & Compliance

Integrated platform to register and track your corporate records in a central location. Effectively manage all your entities, subsidiaries and organizational structures with a complete and secure solution.

## Fonctionnalités

A complete solution to manage all your entities and subsidiaries
Powerful tools to centralize and optimize the management of your organizational structure

## Éléments

- **Hierarchical Management** : Visualization and management of organizational structure with complete tree of entities, subsidiaries and hierarchical relationships.
- **Centralized Records** : Centralized storage of all documents and information related to each entity with advanced search and organization by categories.
- **Obligation Tracking** : Tracking of legal and regulatory obligations by entity with automatic alerts and reminders for important deadlines.
- **Dashboards** : Customizable dashboards to visualize the status of all your entities with key indicators and real-time metrics.
- **Compliance** : Regulatory compliance management with tracking of certifications, audits and controls for each entity.
- **Contact Management** : Centralized directory of contacts and responsible parties for each entity with role and permission management.
