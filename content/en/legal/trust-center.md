# Trust Center

**Dernière mise à jour :** January 1, 2025




## Introduction


## Security of our services


### Security measures


## Éléments

- Data encryption in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256)
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) for all access
- 24/7 system monitoring and threat detection
- Regular penetration testing by independent third parties
- Role-based access management (RBAC)
- Automatic backups and disaster recovery plans
### Security certifications


## Éléments

- ISO 27001 - Information security management system
- ISO 27018 - Personal data protection in the cloud
- SOC 2 Type II compliance
## Regulatory compliance


### Regulations


## Éléments

- GDPR — Full compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation
- French Data Protection Act — Compliance with French regulations
- Digital Services Act — Compliance with the new European regulation
- ISO 27001 — Information security management
### Audits and assessments

We regularly conduct internal and external compliance audits to ensure our continued adherence to standards. Audit reports are available upon request for our clients.
