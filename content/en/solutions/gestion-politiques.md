# Policy Management: Review, create, deploy and administer your policies

**Catégorie :** Ethics & Compliance

Review, create, deploy and administer corporate policies quickly and easily. Centralize the management of all your policies with a complete solution that facilitates policy creation, review, approval and communication.

## Fonctionnalités

A complete solution to manage all your corporate policies
Powerful and intuitive solutions for every aspect of policy management

## Éléments

- **Policy Creation** : Create policies with integrated editor, predefined templates and writing guides to ensure consistency.
- **Approval Workflow** : Customizable workflow for policy approval with defined steps, notifications and validation tracking.
- **Deployment & Communication** : Deploy and communicate your policies with automatic notifications, centralized access and reading confirmation tracking.
- **Review & Updates** : Review planning with automatic alerts, version management and complete modification history.
- **Search & Organization** : Advanced search in all your policies with organization by categories, tags and multiple filters.
- **Compliance & Reporting** : Compliance tracking with reports on active policies, review deadlines and acceptance rates.
