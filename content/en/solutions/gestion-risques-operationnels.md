# Operational Risk Management: Identify, assess and mitigate your risks

**Catégorie :** Risk Management

Identification, assessment and mitigation of risks that could impact your organization's objectives. A complete solution to effectively manage all your operational risks with structured processes and modern tools.

## Fonctionnalités

A complete suite of tools to effectively manage your operational risks
Powerful solutions to identify, assess and mitigate all your risks

## Éléments

- **Risk Identification** : Systematic identification of operational risks with complete taxonomy and support for different types of organizational risks.
- **Assessment & Scoring** : Quantitative and qualitative risk assessment with customizable risk matrices and automatic scoring according to your criteria.
- **Dynamic Heatmap** : Interactive risk visualization with real-time mapping, advanced filters and customizable views by department or process.
- **Mitigation Plans** : Planning and tracking of mitigation actions with responsible assignment, deadline tracking and automatic notifications.
- **Risk Indicators** : Tracking of key risk indicators (KRI) with automatic alerts, trends and predictive analyses to anticipate emerging risks.
- **Reporting & Compliance** : Generation of personalized reports with predefined templates, Excel/PDF exports and automatic scheduling for compliance.
